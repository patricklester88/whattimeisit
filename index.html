What is WhoAmI?

WhoAmI is a simple anonymous identity idea. You can create a unique ID, print it, save it, or share it using a QR code.

It can be used for fun, introductions, luggage labels, anonymous contact cards, family history pages, memorial pages, or future verified profiles.

Proof Levels

Level 1: Self-certified — you say you are you.

Level 2: Witnessed — someone else confirms they know you.

Level 3: Verified — stronger identity checks may be added later.

Privacy

This first version does not store personal information. The ID is generated in your browser. No account is required.

Contact
